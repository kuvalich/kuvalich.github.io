Features

Everything you need

Powerful tools, zero complexity.

Your Photos

Add photos from your gallery. Pinch to zoom and drag to reposition the image right on the card — what you see is what you get.

Themes & Borders

Multiple colour themes — Warm Parchment, Midnight Copper, Obsidian Ink and more — plus scenic backgrounds like Sunset, Forest, and Ocean.

Flexible Layout

Swap the photo to either side with a single tap. The 12-month wall layout supports two independent photo slots.

Export JPG & PDF

Desk calendar exports at 2200×1375 px (A5 @ 300 dpi). Wall poster exports at 2400×3600 px (A3 @ 300 dpi). Both print-shop ready.

Save & Reload Designs

Name and save your favourite designs. Reload them any month to instantly generate next month's calendar — zero rework.

Notes & Writing Lines

Add a short quote or caption. Toggle ruled writing lines on or off — perfect for hand-writing notes on a printed copy.

Special Dates

Pin important birthdays and anniversaries to your wall poster. They appear as a highlighted events list at the bottom of the calendar.

100% Private

Everything stays on your device. No account, no cloud, no tracking. Your photos and designs never leave your phone.

Two Formats

Pick your format

Both formats export at print-shop quality.

Tabletop Desk Calendar

A5 landscape layout with a photo on one side and the monthly grid on the other. Export as JPG (2200×1375 px) or PDF. Flip the photo to either side with one tap.

Wall Poster Calendar

A3 portrait poster in 1-month, 6-month, or full 12-month layouts. Add up to two personal photos, set a scenic theme, pin special dates, and export at 2400×3600 px.

How it works

Ready in minutes

1

Choose a format

Pick Tabletop Desk or Wall Poster from the home screen.

2

Add photo & style

Select from your gallery. Pinch-zoom to frame the shot, then pick a theme.

3

Set the month

Tap directly on the preview or use the Date panel. Add special dates for wall calendars.

4

Export & print

Save as JPG or PDF and take it straight to a print shop — or print at home.
