Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026  ·  Effective immediately

The short version: Calender Studio does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Everything stays on your device.

1. Who we are

Calender Studio is developed by SGS Infotech. This policy explains what data (if any) we handle when you use the app.

2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, transmit, or store:

  • Your name, email, or any account information
  • Photos or images you use in the app
  • Calendar designs you create or save
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers

3. Local storage only

All data — including saved designs and preferences — is stored locally on your device using SQLite (Room database). This data never leaves your device and we have no access to it.

4. Photos & gallery access

The app requests permission to read photos from your device gallery solely to let you add images to your calendar designs. Photos are processed locally and never uploaded anywhere.

5. Third-party services

Calender Studio does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. There are no SDKs that phone home.

6. Exported files

When you export a JPG or PDF, the file is saved to your device's local storage (Photos or Documents). We have no access to these files. Sharing is entirely your choice.

7. Children's privacy

Calender Studio does not knowingly collect information from children under 13. Since we collect no data at all, this policy applies equally to all users.

8. Changes to this policy

If we ever introduce any data collection (for example, optional cloud sync in a future version), we will update this policy and notify you within the app before collecting any data.

9. Contact
