1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how the 2048 mobile game (“the App”, “we”, “our”) handles your information. We built 2048 to be a simple, offline puzzle experience, and we respect your privacy. In short: the App does not collect, sell, or share your personal data.

2. Information We Collect

2048 does not collect, transmit, or store any personally identifiable information on our servers. We do not require an account, sign-in, or any contact details to play. We do not run analytics or tracking SDKs.

3. Local Storage

To make the game enjoyable, the App saves a small amount of data locally on your device only, including:

  • Your best score
  • Your selected color theme and clock format preference

This information never leaves your device and is automatically removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data.

4. Device Permissions

2048 is designed to work fully offline and does not request access to your location, contacts, camera, microphone, or files. The App does not require an internet connection to function.

5. Third-Party Services

The current version of the App does not include third-party advertising, analytics, or social media integrations. If this changes in a future release, this policy will be updated and the relevant providers will be listed here along with links to their own privacy policies.

6. Children's Privacy

2048 is suitable for users of all ages and does not knowingly collect any personal information from anyone, including children under the age of 13. Because no data is collected, no personal information from children is ever stored or processed.

7. Data Security

Since your gameplay data stays on your device and is not transmitted to us, it benefits from your device's own security protections. We encourage you to keep your device's operating system up to date.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the App or legal requirements. When we do, we will revise the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. Continued use of the App after changes take effect constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.

9. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out at a123developer@gmail.com.
