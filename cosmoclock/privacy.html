Privacy Policy

Effective Date: May 27, 2026  ·  Last Updated: May 27, 2026

Overview

Cosmo Clock is designed with your privacy as a priority. We do not collect, store, or sell your personal data. The app functions entirely on your device.

This Privacy Policy describes how Cosmo Clock ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application available on Google Play. By using Cosmo Clock, you agree to the practices described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Cosmo Clock collects no personal information from users. Specifically:

  • We do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or any other personally identifiable information.
  • We do not require account creation or registration.
  • We do not track your location.
  • We do not access your contacts, messages, photos, or files.
  • We do not collect usage analytics or behavioral data.

Data Stored Locally on Your Device

Cosmo Clock stores the following preferences locally on your device using Android's SharedPreferences storage. This data never leaves your device:

  • Selected Theme — which of the 4 visual themes you have chosen.
  • Time Format — whether you prefer 12-hour or 24-hour time display.

This data can be cleared at any time by uninstalling the app or clearing app data in your device settings.

Permissions

Cosmo Clock requests only the permissions necessary for its core features:

  • Audio playback — required to play the ambient sleep soundscapes. Audio is generated synthetically on-device and no recordings are accessed or stored.
  • Keep screen on — allows the clock display to remain active while in use as a bedside clock.

We do not request internet access, storage, camera, microphone, or location permissions.

Advertising

Cosmo Clock may display banner advertisements served by Google AdMob. Google AdMob may collect certain device identifiers and usage data to serve relevant ads. This is governed by Google's Privacy Policy, which you can review at policies.google.com/privacy.

You can opt out of personalized advertising through your device's ad settings at any time.

Third-Party Services

Cosmo Clock may use the following third-party services:

  • Google AdMob — for in-app advertising. Subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play Services — for app distribution and updates.

We do not integrate any other third-party analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs.

Children's Privacy

Cosmo Clock does not knowingly collect any personal information from children under 13 years of age. Since we collect no personal data at all, the app is safe for general audiences. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, please contact us and we will take appropriate action.

Data Security

Since Cosmo Clock does not transmit personal data over the internet, there is no risk of a data breach involving your personal information. Your theme preferences and time format settings remain exclusively on your device.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the app after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
